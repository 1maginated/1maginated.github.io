How the work gets made

Process

Frame by frame, beat by beat

Each edit starts with a beat map — the track broken down hit by hit, so every cut, flash and camera move lands exactly on the audio. Then it's masking, twixtor-style time remaps, hand-drawn transitions and color, layered over 10–15 hours in DaVinci Resolve.

Everything ships in native 4K. That's why the retention holds: viewers watch 92% of an edit on average, and the like ratio sits above 99%.
